For **FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28)** under the **Finance Act 2026**, individual minimum tax is **not area-based**: it is a flat **Tk 5,000** for an individual whose total income exceeds the applicable tax-free threshold, with **Tk 1,000 for a first-time/new taxpayer**. For **FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)** under the **Finance Ordinance 2025**, the same flat **Tk 5,000 / Tk 1,000** rule applied; the former area-based **Tk 5,000 / Tk 4,000 / Tk 3,000** structure was discontinued from that year.[1][2][3]

## FY 2026-27 — Finance Act 2026

For a general resident individual/HUF, the Finance Act 2026 schedule provides:[1][4]

| Band of total income | Rate |
|---|---:|
| First Tk 4,00,000 | 0% |
| Next Tk 3,00,000 | 10% |
| Next Tk 4,00,000 | 15% |
| Next Tk 5,00,000 | 20% |
| Next Tk 20,00,000 | 25% |
| Remaining income | 30% |

- General tax-free threshold: **Tk 4,00,000**.[1][4]
- Enhanced thresholds include **Tk 4,50,000** for women and taxpayers aged 65+, **Tk 5,25,000** for disabled and third-gender taxpayers, and **Tk 5,75,000** for qualifying freedom-fighter categories.[1]
- **Minimum tax:** **Tk 5,000 flat, nationwide**, where total income exceeds the threshold; **Tk 1,000** for first-time taxpayers. It operates as a floor—normal slab tax applies if higher.[1][4][5]
- The NBR’s official Finance Act 2026 page lists the Act as enacted on 6 July 2026; the practitioner briefing identifies the changes as arising from **Schedule 1** of that Act.[1][6]

## FY 2025-26 — Finance Ordinance 2025

For AY 2026-27, the Finance Ordinance 2025 raised the general threshold to **Tk 3,75,000** and removed the former 5% entry slab.[2][3]

| Band of total income | Rate |
|---|---:|
| First Tk 3,75,000 | 0% |
| Next Tk 3,00,000 | 10% |
| Next Tk 4,00,000 | 15% |
| Next Tk 5,00,000 | 20% |
| Next Tk 20,00,000 | 25% |
| Remaining income | 30% |

- The Ordinance’s enhanced thresholds included **Tk 4,25,000** for women/seniors, **Tk 4,75,000** for disabled and third-gender taxpayers, and **Tk 5,25,000** for qualifying freedom fighters.[2]
- **Minimum tax:** a **flat Tk 5,000**, not dependent on taxpayer location; **Tk 1,000** for new taxpayers for AY 2026-27 and AY 2027-28.[2][3]
- Therefore, the old **Tk 5,000 / Tk 4,000 / Tk 3,000** city-corporation versus other-area distinction **did not continue** into FY 2025-26; it was the pre-2025 arrangement.[2][7]

## Direct answer

- **FY 2026-27 / AY 2027-28:** flat **Tk 5,000** everywhere; **Tk 1,000** only for first-time taxpayers; slabs begin after a **Tk 4,00,000** general threshold.[1][4]
- **FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27:** flat **Tk 5,000** everywhere; **Tk 1,000** for new taxpayers; slabs begin after a **Tk 3,75,000** general threshold.[2][3]
- **Area-based Tk 5,000 / Tk 4,000 / Tk 3,000:** no longer applicable for either year; it was replaced by the flat minimum-tax rule under the Finance Ordinance 2025 and continued under the Finance Act 2026.[1][2][7]

One nuance: the FY2025-26 budget speech initially described a Tk 3,75,000 threshold for both AY 2026-27 and AY 2027-28, but the subsequently enacted Finance Act 2026 raised the general threshold to Tk 4,00,000 for AY 2027-28.[1][8]
