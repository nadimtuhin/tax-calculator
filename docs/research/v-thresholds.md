For **assessment year 2027–28** under Bangladesh’s Finance Act 2026, the individual tax-free thresholds are: general **Tk 400,000**; women and people aged 65+ **Tk 450,000**; persons with disabilities and third-gender taxpayers **Tk 525,000**; and gazetted war-wounded freedom fighters / gazetted July Warriors **Tk 550,000**. The parent/guardian allowance is an **additional Tk 50,000 on top of the parent’s own category threshold**—so a woman parent of a person with a disability would have **Tk 500,000** (Tk 450,000 + Tk 50,000), subject to the statutory conditions.[1][2]

| Taxpayer category | Tax-free income threshold |
|---|---:|
| General taxpayer | Tk 400,000 [1] |
| Woman taxpayer | Tk 450,000 [1] |
| Senior citizen aged 65 or above | Tk 450,000 [1] |
| Person with disability | Tk 525,000 [1] |
| Third-gender taxpayer | Tk 525,000 [1] |
| Gazetted war-wounded freedom fighter | Tk 550,000 [1] |
| Gazetted July Warrior (injured in the July 2024 uprising) | Tk 550,000 [1] |
| Parent/legal guardian of a person with disability | Own category threshold **plus Tk 50,000** [2] |

## Parent allowance: how it works

Yes—the **Tk 50,000 is additive** to the parent’s or legal guardian’s own applicable threshold, not a standalone replacement threshold. Thus:

- General male parent: Tk 400,000 + Tk 50,000 = **Tk 450,000**[1][2]
- Woman parent: Tk 450,000 + Tk 50,000 = **Tk 500,000**[1][2]
- Parent aged 65+: Tk 450,000 + Tk 50,000 = **Tk 500,000**[1][2]
- Parent who is themselves a person with disability: Tk 525,000 + Tk 50,000 = **Tk 575,000**[1][2]
- Third-gender parent: Tk 525,000 + Tk 50,000 = **Tk 575,000**[1][2]
- War-wounded freedom-fighter or July-Warrior parent: Tk 550,000 + Tk 50,000 = **Tk 600,000**[1][2]

The allowance applies for **each dependent child or ward with a disability**; where both parents are taxpayers, **only one parent** may claim the benefit.[2][3]

> “Parents or legal guardians of persons with disabilities will be entitled to an extra Tk 50,000 in tax-free income for each dependent child or ward.”[2]

> “The income tax-free threshold would be increased for parents or legal guardians of each physically challenged child/adopted child by Tk 50,000. If both the parents of a physically challenged child are taxpayers, either of the parents will be entitled to avail the facility.”[3]
