**No — the applicable general tax-free threshold for AY 2026–27 returns is Tk 400,000, not Tk 375,000.** The Tk 375,000 figure was the threshold in the original Finance Bill/budget proposal; the enacted Finance Act 2026 raised it to Tk 400,000 for both AY 2026–27 and AY 2027–28.[1][2][3]

## What NBR is using

NBR has published **“আয়কর পরিপত্র, ২০২৬–২০২৭”** (“Income Tax Paripatra, 2026–2027”) on its official Income Tax Paripatra page; it is the return-filing guidance relevant to AY 2026–27.  The NBR PDF itself could not be text-extracted here, but its publication date is listed as 2 September 2026 and it follows the enacted Finance Act 2026 framework.[4][5]

For a **general individual taxpayer**, the AY 2026–27 slab begins:

> “Up to tax-free threshold* — Nil”  
> “*Tax-free threshold … General taxpayers – BDT400,000”[1]

PwC’s Finance Act 2026 briefing states the same threshold for AY 2026–27:

> “General taxpayers – BDT400,000”  
> “Women, senior citizens aged 65 years or above – BDT450,000”  
> “Persons with disabilities – BDT525,000”  
> “War-wounded gazette freedom fighters and July gazette fighters – BDT550,000”  
> “Third-gender taxpayers – BDT525,000”[1]

PwC’s current individual tax summary likewise says: “The basic exemption limit for general taxpayers is BDT 400,000,” in its table covering FY 2025/26 and FY 2026/27.[3]

## Exact AY 2026–27 thresholds

| Taxpayer category | Tax-free threshold for AY 2026–27 |
|---|---:|
| General individual | Tk 400,000 [1] |
| Women and senior citizens aged 65+ | Tk 450,000 [1] |
| Persons with disabilities | Tk 525,000 [1] |
| Third-gender taxpayers | Tk 525,000 [1] |
| War-wounded gazetted freedom fighters / July gazette fighters | Tk 550,000 [1] |

The taxable bands above the threshold for AY 2026–27 are 10% on the next Tk 300,000; 15% on the next Tk 400,000; 20% on the next Tk 500,000; 25% on the next Tk 2,000,000; and 30% on the remainder.[1]

## Why Tk 375,000 is wrong for the return

The Tk 375,000 amount was announced in the budget-stage reporting as the proposed threshold for FY 2026–27/FY 2027–28, with correspondingly lower category thresholds.  But the final Finance Act 2026 changed the general threshold to Tk 400,000; PwC’s table explicitly labels the AY 2026–27 threshold “General taxpayers – BDT400,000.”  The Daily Star also reported the revised enacted position: “the tax-free threshold has been set at Tk 400,000 for FY2026-27 and FY2027-28,” replacing the earlier proposed Tk 375,000.[1][2][6]

So, for an individual filing the AY 2026–27 return by 30 November 2026 for income earned from July 2025 to June 2026, use **Tk 400,000** as the general exemption threshold.[1][3]
