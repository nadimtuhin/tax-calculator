For **FY 2026–27 (income year 1 July 2026–30 June 2027; assessment year 2027–28)**, the enacted **Finance Act 2026** raised the general tax-free threshold to **Tk 400,000**, removed location-based minimum tax in favour of a flat **Tk 5,000** floor, and cut the investment-rebate cap from Tk 10 lakh to **Tk 7.5 lakh**. The NBR lists Finance Act 2026 as enacted on **6 July 2026**.[1][2]

## Tax-free thresholds (AY 2027–28)

| Taxpayer category | Tax-free income |
|---|---:|
| General taxpayer | Tk 400,000 |
| Female taxpayer | Tk 450,000 |
| Senior citizen aged 65+ | Tk 450,000 |
| Person with disability | Tk 525,000 |
| Third-gender taxpayer | Tk 525,000 |
| Gazetted war-wounded freedom fighter / gazetted July Warrior | Tk 550,000 |
| Parent or legal guardian of a person with disability | Additional Tk 50,000; where both parents are taxpayers, only one may claim it |

These are the enacted AY 2027–28 thresholds; the same thresholds also apply for AY 2026–27 under the Finance Act 2026 presentation.[2][3][4]

## Tax slabs and rates

For a resident individual (or non-resident Bangladeshi citizen), after the applicable tax-free threshold:[2][3]

| Income band | Rate |
|---|---:|
| Up to applicable tax-free threshold | Nil |
| Next Tk 300,000 | 10% |
| Next Tk 400,000 | 15% |
| Next Tk 500,000 | 20% |
| Next Tk 2,000,000 | 25% |
| Remaining income | 30% |

The 5% entry slab that existed before FY 2025–26 remains abolished; the first taxable band is 10%. A 35% top rate is scheduled only from AY 2028–29 onward, not for AY 2027–28.[2][5]

## Minimum tax

If total income exceeds the applicable tax-free threshold, the minimum tax is:

- **Tk 5,000** for general taxpayers, nationwide.
- **Tk 1,000** for a first-time taxpayer.

The former area-based tiers—Tk 5,000 in Dhaka North/Dhaka South/Chattogram City Corporation, Tk 4,000 in other city corporations, and Tk 3,000 elsewhere—no longer differentiate the minimum tax for FY 2026–27; the flat Tk 5,000 rule applies regardless of location.[3][6][7]

## Salary exemption

For salaried individuals, the exempt portion of salary income is the **lower of**:

- One-third of salary income; or
- **Tk 500,000**.

This Tk 500,000 ceiling was introduced for FY 2025–26 and continues for FY 2026–27.[8][9]

## Investment tax rebate

The rebate is the **lowest** of:[2][5][9]

- 3% of taxable income;
- 10% of eligible investment/expenditure;
- Tk 750,000.

Eligible items include recognized provident-fund contributions—now subject to a recognition requirement—DPS, life-insurance premiums, Sanchayapatra, and qualifying securities; the former Tk 500,000 limit on unit certificates, mutual funds, ETFs, and collective investment schemes was removed.[2]

## Wealth surcharge

Surcharge applies to resident individuals on regular tax, based on net wealth or specified asset ownership:[3][4]

| Net wealth / condition | Surcharge |
|---|---:|
| Up to Tk 4 crore | Nil |
| Above Tk 4 crore to Tk 10 crore; or more than one motor car; or house property exceeding 8,000 sq ft | 10% |
| Above Tk 10 crore to Tk 20 crore | 20% |
| Above Tk 20 crore to Tk 50 crore | 30% |
| Above Tk 50 crore | 35% |

From FY 2026–27, the minimum tax is excluded from the surcharge base.[10]

## Changes versus FY 2025–26

| Item | FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27 | FY 2026–27 / AY 2027–28 |
|---|---:|---:|
| General threshold | Tk 375,000 | Tk 400,000 |
| Women / 65+ | Tk 425,000 | Tk 450,000 |
| Disabled / third gender | Tk 500,000 | Tk 525,000 |
| War-wounded freedom fighter / July Warrior | Tk 525,000 | Tk 550,000 |
| Parent of disabled child | Extra Tk 50,000 | Extra Tk 50,000 |
| Slabs | 10/15/20/25/30% | Same |
| Minimum tax | Flat Tk 5,000; Tk 1,000 first-time | Same |
| Salary exemption cap | Tk 500,000 | Tk 500,000 |
| Investment rebate | Lowest of 3% of income, 15% of investment, Tk 1,000,000 | Lowest of 3% of income, 10% of investment, Tk 750,000 |

The principal personal-tax changes in Finance Act 2026 are therefore the higher thresholds and the reduced investment-rebate rate and cap; the slab rates, salary-exemption cap, flat minimum tax, and surcharge structure carry forward from FY 2025–26.[2][6][11]

## FY 2025–26: post-budget changes

FY 2025–26 was enacted through the **Finance Ordinance 2025** (3 June 2025), followed by the **Certain Laws Relating to Finance (Amendment) Ordinance 2025** (29 June 2025); NBR’s official Finance Acts page lists both instruments.[1]

The core individual-tax parameters for FY 2025–26—Tk 375,000 general threshold, 10/15/20/25/30% slabs, flat Tk 5,000 minimum tax, and Tk 500,000 salary-exemption ceiling—were as enacted under the Finance Ordinance 2025 and were not reversed by the subsequent amendment ordinance. The later amendment did not change the FY 2025–26 personal threshold, slab rates, minimum tax, or salary-exemption cap.[6][8][12]

One clarification on timing: some press coverage describes the Tk 400,000 threshold as a “revised proposal” because Parliament considered phased increases through FY 2030–31; the enacted Finance Act 2026 confirms **Tk 400,000 for AY 2027–28**, with further increases scheduled for later assessment years.[2][13][14]
